# Transparency Policy

## What this ledger tracks
The public ledger tracks project funds moving in and out of Green Tea Hut #1. Each entry includes date, amount, category, description, status, and a proof/reference link when available.

## Pending vs confirmed
- **Pending**: expected funds or expenses that have been announced but are not fully settled or reconciled.
- **Confirmed**: funds or expenses that have been received, paid, and recorded with supporting confirmation.

## Update cadence
Ledger updates are published at least once per month, with additional updates whenever meaningful funding or spending activity occurs.

## Proof-link policy
Where appropriate, each ledger line includes a proof link (receipt, document, transaction reference, or photo evidence) so the public can verify the recorded activity.
