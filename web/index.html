🎖️ Labor & Services Payout

Clock workers in with signed QR payloads, accrue labor in 15-minute buckets, and record each completed transfer exactly once with proof.

MVP guardrails: one active shift per worker per site, signed QR nonce replay protection, 10-minute QR freshness, and one-time transfer confirmation per shift.

MVP payment handling: Payments are recorded here; transfer is executed externally in MVP.

Status guide: COMMITTED = approved, awaiting transfer. CONFIRMED = transfer completed + proof attached.

Deployment recommendation: use Option 1 for this MVP — keep `ProjectLedger` as the public record of requested/committed/confirmed labor payouts and execute payout settlement off-chain. Option 2 remains a future path if on-chain escrow or ABI-level shift state becomes necessary.

🪪 QR issuance

Reviewer-signed QR payloads carry the site, task, nonce, timestamp, and hourly rate used by the worker clock-in flow.

🕒 Worker clock in

Paste the scanned QR payload and bind it to the worker wallet. Duplicate clock-ins and reused QR nonces are blocked.
